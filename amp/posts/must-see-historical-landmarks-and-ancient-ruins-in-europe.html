You absolutely need to check out the Roman ruins of Mérida in Spain. It's often overlooked by international tourists, but the Roman Theatre and the massive bridge are still remarkably intact and you can actually walk right through them without massive crowds.

Pula Arena in Croatia is an absolute must. It's one of the six largest surviving Roman arenas in the world, and unlike the Colosseum, you can wander around the subterranean levels with almost no one else around.

Don't skip the Megalithic Temples of Malta! Sites like Ħaġar Qim and Mnajdra are older than Stonehenge and the Egyptian pyramids. Standing in front of those massive megaliths overlooking the Mediterranean is a surreal experience.

If your route takes you through France, skip the typical tourist spots and go see the Pont du Gard. It's a massive, three-tiered Roman aqueduct that crosses a river, and seeing the engineering up close without modern barriers everywhere is breathtaking.

Delphi is well-known, but have you heard of Mystras near Sparta? It's a sprawling Byzantine ghost town clinging to the side of a mountain with ruined palaces, churches with faded frescoes, and absolute silence except for the wind.
