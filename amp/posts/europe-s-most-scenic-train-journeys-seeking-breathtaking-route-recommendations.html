Oh, you absolutely HAVE to do the **Glacier Express** in Switzerland! It's dubbed the 'slowest express train in the world' for a reason – it takes its time winding through the most incredible Alpine scenery, over countless bridges and through tunnels. Panoramic windows are a must! Late spring or early autumn offers beautiful colours and good weather.

Seconding Switzerland, but specifically the **Bernina Express**! It's a UNESCO World Heritage route that connects Chur or St. Moritz to Tirano, Italy. The engineering is mind-blowing (think Brusio spiral viaduct), and the views of glaciers, mountains, and valleys are truly breathtaking. Summer is fantastic, but winter with snow is also magical.

If you're going to Norway, the **Flåm Railway** is a short but incredibly steep and dramatic journey from Myrdal down to Flåm, right on the Sognefjord. You'll pass stunning waterfalls (Kjosfossen is a highlight!) and deep gorges. Combine it with a fjord cruise for the ultimate experience. Best in summer for greenery and full water flow.

For a completely different vibe, consider the **West Highland Line** in Scotland, particularly the section from Fort William to Mallaig. It's famous for the Glenfinnan Viaduct (Harry Potter fans, take note!) and offers spectacular views of lochs, glens, and rugged landscapes. Late spring or early autumn usually means good weather and fewer midges!

The **Cinque Terre train** in Italy is a series of short hops between incredibly picturesque coastal villages. The views of the colourful towns clinging to the cliffs and the sparkling Ligurian Sea are unforgettable. It's not a long journey, but the scenery is dense with beauty! Don't overlook the **Semmering Railway** in Austria! It's another UNESCO site and a masterpiece of 19th-century engineering. The route winds through the Austrian Alps, featuring stunning viaducts and tunnels, offering incredible mountain vistas. Summer or early autumn would be ideal for clear views.

A hidden gem: the **Douro Valley Line (Linha do Douro)** in Portugal. This train hugs the Douro River, passing through terraced vineyards and charming villages. It's absolutely stunning, especially in late summer/early autumn during the grape harvest, but spring with everything blooming is also beautiful.

The **GoldenPass Line** in Switzerland offers a fantastic variety of landscapes as it links Lucerne to Montreux. You'll see lakes, mountains, and vineyards through panoramic windows. It's actually three different trains but forms one continuous scenic route. Any time of year is beautiful, but summer is most popular.

The **Bergen Railway** from Oslo to Bergen in Norway is consistently ranked among the world's most scenic. It crosses the vast Hardangervidda mountain plateau, offering incredible, stark beauty, glaciers, and waterfalls. Winter transforms it into a snow-covered wonderland, but summer offers great hiking opportunities.

For amazing coastal views without breaking the bank, take the regional train along the **French Riviera** from Nice towards Ventimiglia (Italy). You get continuous, sparkling Mediterranean views, charming towns like Monaco and Menton, and dramatic cliffs. Spring or early autumn are perfect for pleasant weather and fewer crowds.

My vote goes to the trains leading up to the **Jungfraujoch** in Switzerland from Lauterbrunnen or Grindelwald. While not a 'journey' in the traditional sense, the ascent through the Eiger and Mönch mountains, with views of the Aletsch Glacier and surrounding peaks, is genuinely awe-inspiring. Pick a clear day, any season, for the best experience!
