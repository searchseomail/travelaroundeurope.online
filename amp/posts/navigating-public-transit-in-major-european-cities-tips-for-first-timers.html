In London, honestly just use your contactless credit card or phone (Apple Pay/Google Pay) for everything. Tap in and tap out at every station gates. Do not buy a paper ticket or a Visitor Oyster card unless you really want a souvenir, because contactless automatically calculates the daily fare cap for you.

Berlin is a bit different. While you can use contactless for some things, the BVG app is great for buying tickets, but remember you MUST validate (entwerten) paper tickets in the little orange or yellow machines on the platforms before boarding, otherwise you risk a hefty fine!
