Best river cruises to take for a relaxing European vacation

static_dreamer
Topic Starter

My partner and I are looking to book a peaceful European getaway next spring and want to try a river cruise for the first time. We are mainly hoping to avoid massive crowds, enjoy great food and wine, and just relax while watching scenic landscapes pass by. Which river and cruise line would you recommend for the most tranquil and stress-free experience?
