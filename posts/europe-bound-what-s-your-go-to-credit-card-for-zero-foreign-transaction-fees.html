Europe-Bound! What's Your Go-To Credit Card for Zero Foreign Transaction Fees?

midnight_orbit
Topic Starter
Posted: 09.01.2025 08:07
My partner and I are planning a three-week trip through France, Italy, and Spain this summer, and I'm starting to look into the best ways to manage our finances abroad. We definitely want to avoid those pesky foreign transaction fees, which can really add up over a longer trip. I'm hoping to get some advice on credit cards that offer 0% foreign transaction fees and are widely accepted in Europe. Are there any specific cards you've had great experiences with for travel, perhaps with good rewards for flights or hotels? Any tips on what to look for or avoid would be incredibly helpful!

velvet_vibe
Replied: 19.11.2025 00:12
This is crucial: when paying, the terminal might ask if you want to be charged in your home currency (USD) or the local currency (EUR). ALWAYS choose EUR. If you choose USD, the merchant's bank will do the conversion, and it's almost always at a terrible rate. This is called Dynamic Currency Conversion (DCC) and it's a money trap!
