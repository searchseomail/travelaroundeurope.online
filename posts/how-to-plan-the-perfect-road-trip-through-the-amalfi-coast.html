How to plan the perfect road trip through the Amalfi Coast

silent_echoes
Topic Starter
Posted: 03.01.2025 02:12
My partner and I are renting a car next month to drive the famous Amalfi Coast route from Sorrento to Salerno. We have about five days for the entire trip and want to make sure we don't miss the best viewpoints, hidden beaches, and authentic local restaurants. Given how notoriously narrow and busy the SS163 highway can be, does anyone have tips on the best times of day to drive or advice on parking? I would love a suggested daily itinerary to help us balance the sightseeing without feeling completely rushed.

Drive early in the morning before 8 AM if you want to avoid tour buses and actually find parking. Once 10 AM hits, the SS163 becomes a parking lot itself!

Make sure you book your parking spots in advance in Positano and Amalfi. Trying to find street parking there as a tourist is practically impossible.

Day 1: Start in Sorrento, drive to Positano, stopping at the Madonnina viewpoint. Grab lunch at Chez Black right on the beach.

Day 2: Explore Positano in the morning, then head to Praiano in the afternoon. Visit Fiordo di Furore—it's a stunning hidden spot underneath the bridge.

Day 3: Head to Amalfi town and Ravello. The gardens at Villa Cimbrone in Ravello offer the absolute best view on the entire coast.
