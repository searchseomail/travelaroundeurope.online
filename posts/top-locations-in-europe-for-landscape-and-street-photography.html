Top locations in Europe for landscape and street photography

tempo_shift
Topic Starter

I am planning a month-long photography trip to Europe this autumn and I want to balance both dramatic landscapes and vibrant street scenes. Which cities or regions offer the best mix of stunning natural scenery and rich urban environments for a diverse portfolio? I'd love to hear your personal favorites, recommended gear for the varying conditions, and any hidden gems that aren't completely overrun by tourists yet.

Edinburgh is hands down my favorite spot for this exact mix. You get the moody, gothic architecture and street performers on the Royal Mile, and you are a twenty-minute bus ride away from the dramatic peaks of the Pentland Hills or Arthur's Seat.

Northern Italy is hard to beat in October. Base yourself in Verona or Bergamo; you get incredible street photography in the historic centers, and Lake Como or the Dolomites are just a short trip away for jaw-dropping landscapes. You can easily find hotel deals and arrange car rentals on this website before heading out.

Consider Porto and the Douro Valley in Portugal. Porto offers colorful, gritty street scenes, steep alleys, and wonderful light, while the Douro wine region nearby gives you terraced autumn vineyards framed by rolling hills and misty rivers.

San Sebastián in Northern Spain is an absolute hidden gem for this. You get incredible Basque urban street culture, bustling pintxo bars, and right around the corner are dramatic coastal cliffs and wild Atlantic landscapes.

For gear, I recommend traveling light: a 35mm or 50mm prime for the streets and a versatile 24-70mm zoom for landscapes. Don't forget a sturdy travel tripod and a good circular polarizer for autumn foliage and reflections.

Ljubljana and Lake Bled in Slovenia should definitely be on your list. Ljubljana is a charming, compact capital with amazing pedestrian street life, and Lake Bled plus the Triglav National Park are a landscape photographer's dream in autumn.
