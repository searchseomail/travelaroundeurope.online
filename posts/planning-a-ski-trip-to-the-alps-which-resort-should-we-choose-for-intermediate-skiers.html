Planning a ski trip to the Alps: Which resort should we choose for intermediate skiers?

solar_tide
Topic Starter
Posted: 06.01.2025 00:49
Hey everyone, a group of four intermediate skiers and snowboarders are planning a week-long winter trip to the Alps this coming January. We are looking for a resort that offers a good mix of reliable snow, extensive red runs, and a lively (but not overly wild) après-ski scene. Budget is moderate, and we would ideally prefer somewhere relatively easy to get to from a major airport like Geneva or Zurich. Which destinations would you recommend based on your experiences?
