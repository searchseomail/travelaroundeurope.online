Curating the ultimate European escape: Best boutique hotels and hidden luxury experiences?

cinder_heart
Topic Starter
Posted: 13.02.2025 06:54
I am currently planning a three-week anniversary trip across Europe and want to steer clear of the standard mega-resorts in favor of something much more intimate and exclusive. We are looking for exceptional boutique hotels that offer incredible local character, private Michelin-starred dining experiences, or bespoke chartered tours. Which hidden gems or high-end properties in destinations like Italy, France, or the Swiss Alps would you recommend for a truly unforgettable luxury vacation?
