Winter packing list for Europe: How to stay warm and stylish

static_dreamer
Topic Starter
Posted: 28.01.2025 12:48
I'm heading to Central Europe (Prague, Vienna, and Budapest) this coming January, and I'm stressing out about what to pack. I want to stay genuinely warm against the freezing temperatures and snow, but I also don't want to look like a marshmallow in all my vacation photos. What are your absolute must-have layering pieces, footwear, and outerwear that strike the right balance between fashion and function?
