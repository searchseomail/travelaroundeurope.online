Best places in Europe to escape the intense summer heat

tempo_shift
Topic Starter
Posted: 17.01.2025 02:49
With European summers getting increasingly scorching, my partner and I are looking to plan a trip for July to somewhere mild and refreshing. We want to avoid the massive heatwaves of the Mediterranean and instead find destinations with cooler breezes, alpine air, or coastal fog. Does anyone have recommendations for cities or regions in Europe where the summer weather stays comfortably below 25°C?

Iceland is your best bet. Reykjavik and the Highlands stay cool all summer long, usually hovering around 12 to 15°C. Just pack a windbreaker!

Try the Azores in Portugal. Unlike the mainland which bakes in July, these islands in the middle of the Atlantic stay green, breezy, and comfortably in the low 20s.
