Budget Reality Check: What Did Your 10-Day Europe Trip REALLY Cost?

tempo_shift
Topic Starter
Posted: 31.01.2025 07:18
Hey everyone, I'm planning my first solo trip to Europe next spring and am trying to set a realistic budget for a 10-day adventure. I'm looking at visiting 2-3 cities, perhaps Paris, Rome, and maybe Amsterdam, and flying from the US. I'm not looking for luxury, but also not extreme backpacking – more like comfortable mid-range accommodation, enjoying local food, some museums, and maybe one or two unique experiences. I've seen so many conflicting figures online, so I'd love to hear from people who have actually done a similar trip recently: what was your total expenditure, including flights, accommodation, food, transport, and activities, and what was your biggest unexpected cost?

Hey! I did a very similar trip last May – Paris, Rome, and then flew to Barcelona for a few days. Total cost for 10 days, flying from NYC, was around $3800. This included a $900 round trip flight (booked 4 months in advance), about $120/night for decent Airbnbs, and a good mix of trattorias and cafes. My biggest unexpected cost was definitely spontaneous gelato purchases in Rome – it adds up faster than you think!

Just got back from a 9-day trip hitting Amsterdam and Paris from Chicago. All in, including flights ($1100), mid-range hotels ($140/night average), food, museums, and train between cities, I spent about $4100. The biggest surprise was how much local transport (metro tickets, tram passes) added up in both cities. Definitely look into multi-day passes if you plan on moving around a lot!

I managed to do Paris and Amsterdam for 10 days for about $3200 from Boston. This was by booking flights during an off-peak sale ($750), staying in well-rated guesthouses (~$90/night), and being smart about food (picnics from supermarkets, happy hour deals).
