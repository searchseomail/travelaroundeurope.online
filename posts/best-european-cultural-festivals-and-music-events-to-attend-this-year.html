Best European cultural festivals and music events to attend this year

rogue_element
Topic Starter
Posted: 09.01.2025 15:10
I am planning my vacation time for this year and want to experience some of Europe's most vibrant cultural and music festivals. Ideally, I am looking for a mix of historic celebrations, vibrant street arts, and top-tier music performances happening between late spring and early autumn. Which events are absolute must-visits, and do you have any practical tips for securing tickets and accommodation without breaking the bank?

If you want a mix of incredible music and historic vibes, you seriously have to check out Primavera Sound in Barcelona or Sziget in Budapest. For accommodation, book hostels or private rooms slightly outside the city center well in advance. Also, while planning your European trip, don't forget to check out Must-see historical landmarks and ancient ruins in Europe? to add some daytime sightseeing to your festival itinerary!

Glastonbury is the ultimate music festival, but getting tickets is pure luck. If you miss out, try Rock Werchter in Belgium or Exit Festival in Serbia. They are much easier to get into and offer mind-blowing performances.

For street arts and unique cultural celebrations, the Edinburgh Fringe Festival in August is unbeatable. It is massive, so start looking for Airbnb or student dorm rentals months ahead because prices skyrocket quickly.
