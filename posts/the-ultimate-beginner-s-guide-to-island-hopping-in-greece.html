The ultimate beginner's guide to island hopping in Greece

iron_willow
Topic Starter
Posted: 25.01.2025 10:39
Hey everyone! My partner and I are finally planning our dream trip to Greece for next summer, but we are completely overwhelmed by the sheer number of islands. We have about two weeks and want a good mix of iconic sights, great food, and a bit of relaxation without spending our entire vacation on a ferry. Could anyone share the ultimate beginner's guide or suggest a logical route for a first-timer? Any tips on booking ferries in advance or which island chains are best to start with would be hugely appreciated!
