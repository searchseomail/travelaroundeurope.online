Where to go in Europe to see the best autumn foliage?

amber_drift
Topic Starter
Posted: 02.02.2025 08:49
My partner and I are planning a two-week trip to Europe this October, and we really want to experience some breathtaking autumn foliage. We are trying to decide between regions like Bavaria, the Scottish Highlands, or perhaps somewhere in Scandinavia. Does anyone have recommendations on which destinations offer the most vibrant fall colors and cozy atmospheres during this time of year?

The Scottish Highlands in October are hard to beat! Driving through Glencoe with all the golden and russet tones on the hills is magical. Plus, curling up in a local pub by a fireplace with some whisky is the definition of cozy.

Bavaria is an absolute fairytale in autumn. Neuschwanstein Castle surrounded by fiery red and orange forests looks like something straight out of a storybook. Rent a car and just drive through the Alpine roads.

If you want something truly off the beaten path, look into Europe's hidden gems for fall foliage. Places like the Plitvice Lakes in Croatia or lesser-known valleys in Slovenia offer stunning colors without the massive tourist crowds.

Scandinavia is gorgeous, but keep in mind that peak autumn foliage happens a bit earlier there, usually late September into early October. By mid-October, many trees in Norway and Sweden might already have dropped their leaves.
