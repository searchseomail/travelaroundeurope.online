Is a guided group tour in Europe worth the money?

urban_atlas
Topic Starter
Posted: 08.01.2025 15:01
I'm currently planning my first trip to Europe and feeling quite overwhelmed by all the logistics, from booking trains to finding hotels. Some friends are telling me that booking an all-inclusive guided group tour is the best way to see the highlights without the stress, while others say it's a massive waste of money and too restrictive. For those of you who have tried both independent travel and guided tours, are they actually worth the high price tag?
