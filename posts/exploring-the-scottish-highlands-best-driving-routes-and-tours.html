Topic Starter

My partner and I are planning a 7-day road trip through the Scottish Highlands next month and want to make sure we hit the most scenic spots. We are thinking about starting in Inverness and tackling the North Coast 500, but we've also heard great things about the Isle of Skye and Glencoe. What are your absolute favorite driving routes, and do you recommend self-driving or booking a guided tour for the trickier single-track roads?
