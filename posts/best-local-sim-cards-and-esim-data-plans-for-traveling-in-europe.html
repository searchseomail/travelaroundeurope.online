Best local SIM cards and eSIM data plans for traveling in Europe

urban_atlas
Topic Starter

I'm heading to Europe next month for a three-week trip spanning France, Italy, and Switzerland, and I'm trying to figure out the most cost-effective way to stay connected. Should I buy a physical local SIM card upon arrival at the airport, or is it better to pre-install an eSIM like Airalo or Vodafone? I need reliable data for navigation and messaging, so any recommendations on carriers or specific data packages would be hugely appreciated!
