Topic Starter

Hey everyone! My partner and I are planning our next big vacation and we really want to minimize our carbon footprint this time around. We are looking for European destinations that truly prioritize sustainability, have great public transport, and offer eco-friendly accommodation options. Does anyone have personal experiences or hidden gems they could recommend that avoid mass tourism?

You should definitely look into Ljubljana, Slovenia! It is one of the greenest capitals in Europe. The entire city center is car-free, public transit is super efficient, and there are so many eco-certified hotels and guesthouses.
