The ultimate guide to wine tasting tours in France

prism_drift
Topic Starter
Posted: 14.01.2025 14:34
Planning a trip to France next spring and I really want to dive deep into the country's best wine regions without feeling rushed. I am trying to figure out whether I should base myself in Bordeaux or Burgundy for a first-timer, or if branching out to the Rhône Valley or Alsace is a better move. Could someone share the ultimate guide or itinerary tips for booking vineyard tours, handling wine shipping back home, and general etiquette for cellar door tastings?

As a first-timer, starting in Burgundy is amazing if you love Pinot Noir and Chardonnay, but Bordeaux is much easier to navigate with public transport and larger estates. If you are coming from outside the EU, definitely check this Schengen Visa guide before booking your flights!
phantom_scribe
Replied: 18.05.2025 13:17
For cellar door etiquette, always make sure to book your appointments at least a few weeks in advance. Walk-ins are rarely accepted at the top Châteaux in Bordeaux or the prestigious Domaines in Burgundy.
tempo_shift
Replied: 17.06.2025 08:00
Alsace is heavily underrated for a spring trip! The wine route is gorgeous, the food has this amazing French-German fusion, and it feels much less crowded than the major Bordeaux hubs.

When it comes to shipping wine back home, most reputable estates will handle the paperwork and logistics for you via specialized freight companies, though it can get quite pricey.
