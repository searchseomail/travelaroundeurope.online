Packing light for 2 weeks in Europe this summer - carry-on only tips?

bold_narrative
Topic Starter
Posted: 15.01.2025 07:25
I'm heading to Italy and Spain for two weeks this July and I really want to avoid checked baggage fees and lost luggage. Has anyone successfully managed a European summer trip using just a carry-on suitcase and a personal item? I'd love to know your essential packing lists, favorite travel-friendly clothing brands, and laundry strategies to keep things light.

Yes, it is totally doable! I did three weeks in Italy last summer with just a 40L backpack and a daypack. The key is merino wool clothing because you can wear it multiple times without it holding odors. If you are planning your itinerary, check out this guide on Mediterranean Magic: Your Top Picks for Most Beautiful Coastal Towns for some amazing destination inspiration.

I swear by packing cubes. They compress everything down and keep your clothes organized. I brought five days' worth of clothes and just did sink wash every few nights using travel detergent sheets. Quick-dry fabrics are an absolute lifesaver in the July heat.

Wear your bulkiest items on the plane—your heaviest sneakers, a light jacket, and jeans. Once you arrive in Italy and Spain, you'll be glad you don't have to lug a giant suitcase over cobblestones and up ancient staircases.
